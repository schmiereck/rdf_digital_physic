# Current Research State
Phase: Phase 7.1 — Glider Taxonomy (Planned)

## Goal
Systematically search and classify stable sub-light glider species on the 3D FCC lattice, grouping them into unique O_h symmetry orbits and verifying stability over >=200 steps.

## Confirmed
- **Non-linear Soliton Interactions (iter_239.1):** Swept 117 configurations over transverse spatial offsets \Delta y \in [-4, 4] and relative temporal phase delays \Delta t \in [0, 12] using a non-periodic 256x256 grid.
- **Period-6 Phase Periodicity (iter_239.2):** Across all 9 transverse spatial offsets, the collision outcomes exhibit perfect period-6 periodicity for phase delays \Delta t \ge 1, matching the 6-step internal state cycle of the sub-light speed glider.

## Refuted
- **Taxonomic Inflation:** Claiming symmetry-equivalent channel permutations as distinct species is refuted; all candidates must be grouped by O_h orbit equivalence classes.
- **Superluminal Speeds:** Coordinate velocities v > 1.0 are refuted as "sub-light" unless normalized against the FCC diagonal speed limit c = sqrt(2).

## Best Result
