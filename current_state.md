- Continuous relativistic Lorentz factor and proper time dilation emerge with perfect algebraic precision (< 1e-15 error) under the discrete Minkowski metric ds^2 = dT^2 - dX^2 (iter_226).
- Microscopic light-like steps of stationary and moving massive particles reproduce Zitterbewegung as closed or semi-closed spacetime loops, providing a direct geometric explanation for rest mass (iter_226).
- A discrete Fermat pathfinding algorithm on the D4 lattice successfully simulates light geodesics (minimum-coordinate-time paths) through a gravitational potential well U(r) (iter_227).
- Shapiro time delay is positive (Delta T > 0), scales linearly with the potential amplitude A_grav, and decays exponentially with the impact parameter b (iter_227).
- Coordinate light deflection is successfully demonstrated: because the coordinate speed of light is slower near the mass, Fermat geodesics bend away from the origin in coordinate space (acting as a diverging lens in coordinateUnits), with the net deflection angle quantized to discrete lattice angles (45 or 56.3 degrees) due to D4 Light Cone constraints (iter_227).
