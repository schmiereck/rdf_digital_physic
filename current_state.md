# Current Research State
Phase: Phase 7.3 — Antiparticles & O_h Covariance (Null Result Confirmed and Diagnosed)

## Goal
Demonstrate that mass, gravity, time dilation, and ultimately quantum phenomena emerge as effects of a minimal set of local, reversible binary rules on a highly symmetric grid.

## Confirmed
- **Chirality and Z2 Enantiomorphism (iter_242):** LUT-08 has chiral charge alternating between -4.0 (even) and +2.0 (odd). Spatial reflection negates chirality.
- **100% Elastic Collisions & Additive Conservation (iter_242):** 10/10 opposite-chirality collisions conserve bit count, chirality sum, and sub-lattice parities.
- **Antiparticle (P-reflected enantiomer) exists and is stable (iter_245, iter_246):** The x-reflected LUT-08 glider propagates stably under the forward LUT-08 rule with opposite chirality (constructional result guaranteed by O_h symmetry). Confirmed stable on L=64 (iter_246).
- **Opposite-chirality collisions are perfectly elastic in axis-aligned configuration (iter_245, iter_246):** 5/5 impact-parameter configurations produce Elastic scattering on L=32. Unrotated head-on collision is Elastic on L=64 (iter_246, sub-goal 246.1).
- **Same-chirality collisions are destructive (iter_245):** 5/5 same-chirality collision configurations produce Partial (3/5) or Chaotic (2/5) outcomes on L=32. Confirmed Partial on L=64 (iter_246).
- **Annihilation hypothesis REFUTED (iter_245):** Opposite-chirality (particle-antiparticle) collisions do NOT annihilate; they scatter elastically. The P-reflected enantiomer is NOT a matter-antimatter partner in the annihilation sense.
- **O_h non-covariance is NOT a boundary artifact (iter_246):** The non-covariance (Elastic → Chaotic under O_h rotation) persists on L=64 grid, refuting the boundary-artifact hypothesis (F1 REFUTED).
- **O_h non-covariance IS a definitional alignment mismatch (iter_246):** Per F4-enhanced criterion, the non-covariance is classified as a coordinate-rounding artifact. The discrete FCC lattice cannot faithfully represent rotated configurations of the LUT-08 glider (velocity [0.25, -0.5, 1.0] is non-axis-aligned), causing sub-lattice phase shifts and different effective impact parameters. The CA rule itself IS O_h-symmetric (by construction), but collision outcomes are geometry-dependent.
- **Elastic collisions are rare narrow windows (iter_246):** Full 24-rotation sweep on L=64: 2/24 Elastic (identity + tid=14), 21/24 Chaotic, 1/24 Partial.
- **Unrotated L=32 results reliable on L=64 (iter_246):** F2 NOT refuted — the unrotated Elastic outcome is robust on L=64, confirming the iter_245 collision catalog is valid for axis-aligned configurations.
- **All solo stability controls pass on L=64 (iter_246):** Rotated gliders remain valid LUT-08 particles (F3 NOT refuted).

## Refuted
- **Boundary-artifact hypothesis (iter_246):** REFUTED (F1). Non-covariance persists on L=64.
- **Annihilation hypothesis (iter_245):** REFUTED. Opposite-chirality collisions scatter elastically, not annihilating.
- **O_h covariance of collision dynamics for non-axis-aligned gliders (iter_245, iter_246):** REFUTED at the outcome level, but classified as definitional alignment mismatch (coordinate-rounding artifact) rather than a fundamental rule failure.
- **F5 (multi-rotation consistency with matching sub-lattice phases) (iter_246):** NOT CONFIRMED. No proper rotation other than identity produces Elastic with matching sub-lattice phases.

## Best Result
- **Complete Phase 7.3 experiment suite (iter_245 + iter_246):** Null result on annihilation with full falsification protocol. O_h non-covariance diagnosed as definitional alignment mismatch with coordinate-rounding evidence.
- **Multi-rotation anisotropy map (iter_246):** 24 proper O_h rotations tested on L=64, providing complete coverage of the rotation group.
