Phase: Focused Exploration

## Goal
Evolve a rule that produces sustained, coherent motion in a pre-structured "ash" environment.

## Confirmed
- **Remnant Composition:** The stable, 80-bit remnant from `rule_011` is composed of 37 objects of 8 distinct types: 5 still-lifes and 3 oscillators. The field is dominated by 29 single-bit still-lifes (iter_133).
- **Pattern Transformation:** The evolutionary process found a rule (`iter_131/rule_011`) that transforms a static "ash" pattern into this stable field of oscillators (iter_131, 132).
- **Remnant is Not a Glider:** The 80-bit remnant is a collection of stable oscillators, not a translating glider (iter_132).
- **Optimal Rule Density:** C2-symmetric rules with medium density (8 kernel pairs) are most likely to produce viable, non-chaotic, sustained motion (iter_129).

## Refuted
- The evolutionary lineage from the flawed early-displacement metric is a dead end (iter_127).
- Hybrid "cooling" and "birth" rules are dominated by chaos (iter_117).
- A two-stage simulation process fails to animate the ash (iter_118, 119).
- Direct searches for simple gliders from small seeds are ineffective (iter_006-096).

## Open Questions
- Can we visualize the remnant's oscillators to understand their spatial distribution and dynamics?
- Can we evolve a *new* rule that specifically animates this stable field of oscillators?
- What happens if two remnants are placed next to each other under `rule_011`?
- Is the remnant's formation deterministic across different random seeds for the initial soup?
