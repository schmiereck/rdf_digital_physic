Phase: 3 - Emergent Dynamics

### Goal
Observe deterministic, bit-conserving scattering in a 2D hexagonal grid.

### Status
FUNDAMENTALLY BLOCKED on glider discovery. All tested paradigms for finding *elemental* moving particles from small, contiguous seeds have failed. The research is now focused on finding *emergent* motion from the interaction of known-stable stationary objects.

### Confirmed Findings
- A principled method exists for generating reversible rule kernels with various symmetries and conservation properties.
- The non-conserving rule (A=3↔B=14) supports a variety of stable, multi-bit, stationary "still life" objects from 3-bit and 4-bit seeds (iter_068, iter_074).
- Symmetric, collinear interaction of two 3-bit still lifes with a **1-cell gap** results in a stable 6-bit still life (iter_075).
- Symmetric, collinear interaction of two 3-bit still lifes with a **2-cell gap** results in no interaction; the objects remain two separate still lifes (iter_076).

### In Progress
- **iter_077:** Testing if an **asymmetric** arrangement of two 3-bit still lifes can break the static equilibrium and produce a composite glider.
