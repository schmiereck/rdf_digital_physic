An elegant and rigorous next step for the research project is to execute **Phase 7.3: Antiparticles**. 

### Scientific Context and Rationale
In the previous phase (Phase 7.2 / iter_242), we mathematically defined and verified the chirality and sub-lattice parity charges of the stable 4-bit sub-light glider `LUT-08` on the 3D Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) lattice. We proved that these charges are conserved in elastic collisions.

In Phase 7.3, we investigate the discrete spacetime symmetries of this system. In any reversible, local, deterministic framework that mimics physical laws, every particle (stable propagating configuration) must have an antiparticle counterpart related by a discrete symmetry (such as C, P, T, or their combination CPT). 

Because the underlying cellular automaton is strictly bit-conserving, "annihilation" of a particle and its antiparticle cannot mean the destruction of the physical bits themselves. Instead, in analogy to matter-antimatter annihilation into photons, it must manifest as the conversion of the bound-state sub-light gliders ($v < c$) into free, non-interacting "radiation" states—specifically, independent single bits propagating at the maximum grid velocity ($v = 1c$), leaving exactly zero stationary debris or bound-state remnants on the grid.
