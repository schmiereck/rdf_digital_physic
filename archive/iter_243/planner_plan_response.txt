## Discussion and Theoretical Alignment

### 1. Conceptual Framework of Phase 7.3 (Antiparticles & Annihilation)
In our discrete bit-grid universe, we have already established that:
* **Lorentz-like Kinematics** exist for our gliders.
* **Chirality ($\chi$)** acts as a $Z_2$ charge, where spatial reflection $P$ negates the charge ($-\chi(t)$).
* **Sub-lattice occupancy ($\mathbf{q}$)** defines a discrete gauge-like charge that is conserved under elastic collisions.

By moving to Phase 7.3, we address the fundamental question of time-reversibility and CPT symmetry. In any reversible, bit-conserving Cellular Automaton, if a trajectory exists, its time-reversed counterpart must also exist. 
For a propagating glider $G$ moving at velocity $\vec{v}$, its time-reversed counterpart $T(G)$ is a glider propagating at velocity $-\vec{v}$. If we also apply spatial Parity $P$ (reflection), we obtain the CPT-conjugate state (the "antiparticle" $\bar{G}$).

Because the system is strictly bit-conserving, "annihilation" cannot mean that the bits disappear. Instead, it must mean the conversion of **rest-mass** (the localized, sub-light propagating glider state) into **massless gauge radiation** ($v=1c$ single-bit gliders), which disperse to infinity and leave the collision center empty. This is the exact discrete-lattice analog of high-energy electron-positron annihilation ($e^+ e^- \to 2\gamma$).

### 2. Experimental Rigor and Controls
To prevent "success" from post-hoc parameter tuning or lattice artifacts, we establish:
1. **Control Run:** A vacuum propagation of the individual particle and antiparticle to confirm their stability and velocity profiles before collision.
2. **Phase and Impact Parameter Sweeps:** We will systematically sweep all possible relative phases and impact parameters to map the entire cross-section of the interaction (elastic scattering vs. annihilation vs. excitation).
3. **CPT-Reversibility Check:** We will verify that the simulation is numerically reversible to machine precision (or rather, exact bit-level precision, since this is a pure binary engine), proving that the annihilation is a true dynamical process governed by CPT symmetry.
