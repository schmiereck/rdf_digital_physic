## Analysis

### Current State Summary

The critical finding from iter_248 is that **all O_h-symmetric, single-cell additive LUTs produce only monospecific single-bit particles**. LUT-08's "4-bit glider" is actually 4 independent copies of the [5,6] single-bit period-2 particle. The fundamental spectrum under LUT-08 consists of exactly 6 period-2 cycles (5 moving, 1 stationary), and no genuine weight-2 or weight-3 bound structures exist across any tested LUT.

### Root Cause Analysis

The monospecificity arises from **additivity**: in the current LUT construction (`generate_symmetric_lut()`), each weight-k state is mapped by independently applying the weight-1 transpositions to each bit. This means:
- Bits within the same cell never influence each other's transitions
- Two bits starting in the same cell will each follow their own independent trajectory and separate after propagation
- There is no mechanism for bits to "share" a channel or create correlated motion

Under LUT-08's weight-1 mapping (ch0↔ch3, ch1↔ch2, ch4↔ch7, ch5↔ch6, ch8↔ch11, ch9↔ch10), the 66 weight-2 states decompose as:
- **6 fixed points**: {0,3}, {1,2}, {4,7}, {5,6}, {8,11}, {9,10} — both channels belong to the same transposition pair, so swapping both gives the same state
- **30 period-2 cycles**: the remaining 60 states pair up under independent transposition application

### The Non-Additive Break

A non-additive weight-2 mapping would mean that when two bits occupy the same cell, their combined transition differs from applying each bit's transition independently. This creates an **interaction**: bits "feel" each other's presence and redirect. This is the minimal mechanism for binding energy > 0.

The simplest non-additive mutation is a **fixed-point swap**: redirect {5,6}→{4,7} and {4,7}→{5,6}, breaking two additive fixed points into a non-additive period-2 cycle. More aggressive mutations can redirect entire period-2 cycles or create longer cycles.

### Critical Design Considerations

1. **Seeding strategy**: Multi-bit seeds must have ≥2 bits in the same cell to activate the non-additive interaction. Seeds with bits in separate cells would just produce independent single-bit particles (as in LUT-08).

2. **O_h symmetry**: Non-additive mutations must respect O_h orbits of weight-2 channel pairs. The 6 transposition-pair fixed points likely form O_h orbits that must be permuted consistently. If O_h-symmetric non-additive LUTs prove impossible, we must relax O_h symmetry.

3. **Bijectivity verification**: Every non-additive variant must pass strict bijectivity (exactly one pre-image per output state) and bit conservation (Hamming weight in = Hamming weight out).

4. **2D hex parallel check**: The 2D hex v=0.469c glider (iter_222) may or may not be a non-interacting composite. If it's genuine, this tells us the problem is specific to FCC/12-channel structure, not LGCA in general.
