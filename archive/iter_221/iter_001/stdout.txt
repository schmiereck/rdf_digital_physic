## Available Fitness Functions

### 1. `NetDisplacementFitness` (from `src/fitness_functions.py`)

**Name:** `"NetDisplacementFitness"`

**Formula:** `fitness = net_displacement / (1 + final_bb_area)`

**Purpose:** Rewards the Euclidean distance the particle's centre of mass travels from start to end, penalized by the final bounding-box area to counter "puffer" exploits (large diffuse patterns). Bit conservation is enforced.

**Key defenses:**
- **Net displacement** only — stationary oscillators score 0.
- **Bounding-box penalty** — suppresses large puffer patterns.
- **Bit conservation gate** — fitness is 0 if bit count changes.
- Optional `strict_bit_conservation` — enforces exact bit count at *every* step.

**Returns:** `(fitness, metrics_dict)` via `__call__`, or just `metrics_dict` via `evaluate()`.

---

### 2. `SubLightFitness` (from `src/fitness_functions.py`)

**Name:** `"SubLightFitness"`

**Formula:** `fitness = displacement * (1 - 1/T)` where T is the internal period.

**Purpose:** Selects for **sub-light-speed (v<c)** periodic gliders, defeating the v=1c (lattice speed of light) glider exploit.

**Key mechanisms:**
1. **Unwrapped CoM tracking** — handles toroidal grid boundary crossings correctly.
2. **Hard velocity gate** — rejects particles with mean velocity ≥ `v_threshold` (default 0.9), eliminating v=1c gliders.
3. **Internal-period gate & bonus** — detects the smallest repeating configuration period T; T ≤ 1 is rejected outright, and larger T values get multiplied by `(1 - 1/T)`.

**Period factor examples:**

| Period T | Factor (1-1/T) | Meaning |
|----------|----------------|---------|
| 1        | 0.0            | Rejected (v=1c trivial) |
| 2        | 0.5            | v=0.5c |
| 4        | 0.75           | v=0.25c |
| 8        | 0.875          | v=1/8c |

**Default settings:** 1200 simulation steps, late window 600–1000, period window 600–800, max_period=64, v_threshold=0.9.

### 3. `DisplacementConsistencyFitness` (from `src/new_fitness.py`)

**Name:** `"DisplacementConsistencyFitness"`

**Formula:** `fitness = mean_velocity_magnitude / (1 + std_dev_velocity_magnitudes)`

**Purpose:** Rewards **consistent, directional motion** and defeats "drifter" exploits — objects that oscillate in place or random-walk while appearing to travel long distances.

**Key mechanism:**
- Divides simulation into `num_windows` (default 5) equal-duration windows.
- Computes per-window velocity vectors.
- High standard deviation of velocity magnitudes → heavy penalty (drifter behavior).
- Low standard deviation → high fitness (true glider behavior).

**Additional features:**
- **Toroidal grid unwrapping** — correctly handles CoM wrapping across grid boundaries.
- **Leaky bit-conservation score** — applies a continuous fractional penalty (not hard fail) for bit-count changes, preserving evolutionary gradient.
- Optional `strict_conservation` — hard-rejects any bit-count change.
- Optional `max_bit_threshold` and `max_velocity_threshold` — hard rejects.

**Returns:** A single `float` score via `__call__`.
