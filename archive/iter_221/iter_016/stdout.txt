**Summary of the GA fitness flow:**

1. **Evaluate** the entire population → `scores` (list of floats, higher = better).
2. **Select** top-`ELITE_SIZE` chromosomes via `select_top_k` (sort descending by score, keep top-k).
3. **Elitism**: elites are copied directly into the next generation.
4. **Rest of offspring** are generated by choosing a random elite parent and applying `swap_mutate`.
5. **Champion tracking**: the single best chromosome seen across all generations is stored in `champion_chrom`/`champion_fitness` and updated whenever a new high score is found.
6. **Per-generation stats** (`best_fitness` = max, `mean_fitness` = mean) are logged to `gen_log` for CSV output.
