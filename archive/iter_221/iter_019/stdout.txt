## Toroidal Unwrapping — Handled **Before** This Section

**Yes, toroidal unwrapping *is* handled**, but it happens in the **upstream preprocessing** (lines 234–262 of the file). The unwrapping logic:

1. Iterates through all history entries sorted by step
2. For each consecutive pair of COM positions, computes the delta
3. If the delta exceeds half the grid size (64 for a 128-grid), it corrects by ±128:
   ```python
   if dx > 64:    dx -= 128.0
   elif dx < -64: dx += 128.0
   ```
4. The *cumulative* unwrapped positions are stored and replace the raw COMs in the history
5. The downstream windowed displacement calculation (lines 310–365) then operates on these **already-unwrapped** COM values

### Conclusion

The displacement computation **relies on pre-unwrapped COM data**, so toroidal boundary crossings are correctly handled. The simple subtraction in line 330 is valid *because* the COM values coming in are continuous (no 0↔128 jumps). Without the unwrapping step (lines 234–262), a particle crossing a boundary would show an artificial jump of ~128 units in displacement, corrupting the velocity calculation.
