### Summary of the calculations:

| Variable | Calculation | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **dx** | `last_entry["com"][0] - first_entry["com"][0]` | Displacement in x (centroid difference), normalized by step count |
| **dy** | `last_entry["com"][1] - first_entry["com"][1]` | Displacement in y (centroid difference), normalized by step count |
| **velocity_mag** | `sqrt(dx*dx + dy*dy)` (then `/ window_steps`) | Euclidean speed — magnitude of the velocity vector per step |

The logic works in two phases:
1. **Raw displacement**: Compute the raw `dx`/`dy` from the first and last centroid positions in a sliding window, then compute the raw magnitude.
2. **Normalization**: If `window_steps > 0`, divide `dx`, `dy`, and `velocity_mag` by `window_steps` to get **per-step** velocity components and speed. If the window has no step difference, everything is set to 0.0.

The results are appended to `window_velocity_mags` (speed list) and `window_velocity_vectors` (as `(dx, dy)` tuples).
