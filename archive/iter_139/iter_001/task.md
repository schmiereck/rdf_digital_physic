**Goal:** Breed a second generation (Gen-2) of rules from the three viable Gen-1 founders and evaluate if the evolutionary process improves fitness.

**1. Load Gen-1 Elites:**
- The three elite parent rules are located in `archive/iter_138/population/`. Their filenames are `rule_002.json`, `rule_051.json`, and `rule_024.json`.

**2. Implement Breeding and Mutation:**
- Create a new script `src/run_parity_evolution_gen2.py`.
- Write a `crossover` function that takes two parent rules (each with 8 kernel pairs) and creates a child rule by taking 4 distinct kernel pairs from each parent.
- Create a `mutate` function that takes a rule and, with a given probability, replaces one of its kernel pairs with a new, randomly generated, valid, parity-conserving kernel pair.

**3. Generate Gen-2 Population:**
- Create a population of 100 Gen-2 rules and save them to `archive/iter_139/population/`.
- The population should be generated by evenly breeding the three elites:
  - 33 children from crossover of (rule_002, rule_051)
  - 33 children from crossover of (rule_051, rule_024)
  - 34 children from crossover of (rule_024, rule_002)
- Apply mutation to 10% of the generated children (i.e., mutate 10 of the 100 new rules).

**4. Evaluate Gen-2 Population:**
- Evaluate all 100 Gen-2 rules using the identical setup from iter_138:
  - Load the initial state from `src/ash_pattern.json`.
  - Simulate for 200 steps on a 150x150 grid with wrapping boundaries.
  - Calculate fitness using the **local** metric (targeting the two close oscillators) and the **quadratic** bit-ratio penalty (`fitness = displacement / (1 + (bit_ratio - 1)**2)`), with displacement measured between steps 100 and 200.

**5. Analyze and Report:**
- Calculate the mean fitness of the three Gen-1 founders.
- Calculate the mean fitness of the 100 Gen-2 rules.
- Count how many Gen-2 rules surpassed the Gen-1 top score (0.36059).
- Identify the top-performing rule in Gen-2 and its fitness score.
- Output these findings into a final summary YAML block. The YAML should contain: `gen1_mean_fitness`, `gen2_mean_fitness`, `fitness_improvement_pct`, `gen1_top_fitness`, `gen2_top_fitness`, `rules_beating_gen1_top`.
