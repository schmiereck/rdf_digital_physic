## Detailed Research Plan & Context

### 1. Scientific Context
In Phase 4, we successfully constructed the 3D FCC Lattice Gas Cellular Automaton (LGCA) and identified a 4-bit sub-light speed glider designated **LUT-08**. 

However, as highlighted by the Research Manager in the project's **Falsification Protocol**, progress is frequently threatened by **Taxonomic Inflation**—claiming that rotated, reflected, translated, or phase-shifted variations of an existing glider are "new species." To build a true, emergent "Particle Zoo" (Phase 7), we must establish a mathematically rigorous, automated taxonomy framework. 

This experiment (Phase 7.1) aims to systematically search for new glider species on the 3D FCC lattice and classify them using the full **$O_h$ Octahedral Symmetry Group** (order 48).

### 2. Experimental Protocol

#### A. Symmetry Group Representation ($O_h$)
We will write a symmetry checker `src/fcc_symmetry.py` to represent the 48 symmetry operations of the $O_h$ group. Each operation $g \in O_h$ consists of:
1. A permutation of the coordinate axes: $x, y, z \to \pi(x, y, z)$ (6 possible permutations).
2. A sign mapping: $(\pm x, \pm y, \pm z)$ (8 possible sign combinations).

We define the 12 neighbor vectors of the FCC lattice (the vertices of the cuboctahedron):
$$\mathbf{e}_d \in \{ (\pm 1, \pm 1, 0), (\pm 1, 0, \pm 1), (0, \pm 1, \pm 1) \}$$

For each of the 48 operations $g$, we will pre-compute:
- The coordinate mapping $g(\mathbf{r})$ for position offsets.
- The channel permutation matrix $P_g$ mapping each channel $d \to d'$ such that $g(\mathbf{e}_d) = \mathbf{e}_{d'}$.

Two configurations $A$ and $B$ are **O_h-equivalent** if there exists a transformation $g \in O_h$, a spatial translation $\mathbf{t}$, and a temporal phase shift $\tau$ such that:
$$B(t=0) = g(A(t=\tau)) + \mathbf{t}$$

#### B. Search Space and Bounding
We will set up a search engine `src/fcc_glider_search.py` that sweeps localized configurations of active channels:
- **Symmetry-invariant rules:** The reversible, O_h-symmetric FCC rule that supports `LUT-08`.
- **Bounding Box:** Initial bits must fit within a $3 \times 3 \times 3$ grid.
- **Hamming Weight:** $W \le 12$ active bits.
- **Velocity Normalization:** Measured speed $||v||$ must be strictly less than the speed limit $c_{limit} = \sqrt{2}$.

#### C. Evaluation Flow
1. **Simulation Phase:** Each seed is simulated on a sufficiently large grid (e.g., $64 \times 64 \times 64$ with open or periodic boundaries) for 200 steps.
2. **First-Stage Filter:** Eliminate configurations that explode, decay to 0 bits, or remain completely static (average velocity $||v|| = 0$).
3. **Linearity and Periodicity Verification:** Verify that the center of mass moves linearly, and the configuration repeats with some period $T$ (i.e. is periodic in its co-moving frame).
4. **Long-Term Stability Check:** Run surviving candidates for up to 1000 steps to ensure they are true solitons and not long-lived transients.
5. **Orbits Extraction:** Group all valid gliders into equivalence classes under $O_h \times \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}_T$.
6. **Species Comparison:** Verify whether there exists any orbit distinct from that of `LUT-08`.
