Analysis: Why the criterion was not met
---------------------------------------
The iter_200.1 oscillator has a period of exactly 4 steps.  Its centre-of-mass
oscillates between four positions.  The initial position (the L-tromino seed at t=0)
is NOT one of the four oscillation-cycle positions; after a short transient (steps 0-3)
the pattern enters its stable 4-phase cycle and never returns to the exact initial CoM.

CumulativeDisplacementFitness measures the Euclidean distance from the initial CoM (t=0)
to the final CoM (t=200).  Because 200 mod 4 = 0, at step 200 the oscillator is at
phase-0 of its cycle -- CoM (64.333, 63.333), displaced 0.667 cells from the initial
(63.667, 63.333).  The fitness is 0.667/(1+3) = 0.167.

At simulation_steps=50 or 150 (50 mod 4 = 2 -- the phase closest to the initial
position), displacement would be only 0.333 and fitness 0.083 < 0.1.

The iter_200 rule (Rule B) is not a true oscillator but a transient-mover: the pattern
transforms in 2 steps and then becomes static.  Its net displacement is 0.471 cells.

Effectiveness
-------------
A true glider at v=c/3 with 3 bits after 200 steps would displace ~67 cells, giving
fitness ~16.75.  The oscillator's 0.167 provides a ~100x discrimination margin.
CumulativeDisplacementFitness is effective as a fitness discriminator even though
the absolute 0.1 threshold is not met at simulation_steps=200 for this exploit rule.
