Qualitative Analysis:
  The two L-tromino gliders were placed at the same row (row 128)
  but separated by 54 columns (col 100 vs col 154).
  Both gliders carry the same orientation and move East (+row direction)
  under rule g10_rule_001.

  OUTCOME: No interaction detected. The two gliders propagate in
  parallel, maintaining their 54-column lateral separation
  throughout all 300 steps. The bit count remains constant at
  6 (3+3), confirming independent motion with no interaction.

  This demonstrates that at large impact parameters (54 cells),
  same-direction gliders are effectively non-interacting.

Comparison with previous experiments:
  - Head-on collision (iter_180): catastrophic annihilation / non-trivial
    outcome when gliders travel toward each other on the same column
  - Constructive fusion (iter_181): glider + single lone bit fuse into
    a stable 5-bit composite glider
  - Glancing collision (this, impact_param=54): no_interaction_parallel_motion
