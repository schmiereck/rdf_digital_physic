## `src/new_fitness.py` — Definition

The file defines a **`DisplacementConsistencyFitness`** class. Key points:

- **Purpose**: A fitness function that rewards consistent, directional motion (like true gliders) and penalizes "drifter" objects that oscillate or exhibit random-walk behaviour.
- **Core approach**: The simulation is divided into `num_windows` time windows. Per-window velocity vectors (ΔCOM) are computed, and the **standard deviation of velocity magnitudes** is used as a consistency metric.
- **Formula**: `base_fitness = mean_velocity_magnitude / (1 + std_dev_velocity_magnitudes)`
  - High mean velocity + low std dev → high fitness (true gliders)
  - High std dev → suppressed fitness (drifters, even with high mean velocity)
- **Bonus**: Multiplied by a "leaky" bit-conservation score that gives partial credit rather than hard-failing when bit counts change.
- **Parameters**: `num_windows` (default 5) and `bits_per_cell` (default 1).
