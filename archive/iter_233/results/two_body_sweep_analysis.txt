RECOMMENDATIONS — additional sweeps to consider
================================================================================================
  - alpha: optimum is a SHARP interior peak at 2.5; refine the grid between 2.0 and 3.0.
  - threshold: completely flat at the optimum -> no information; widening this axis would be wasteful.
  - gamma: optimum sits on the LOW boundary (0.05); extend the sweep DOWN below 0.05.
  - kappa: optimum sits on the HIGH boundary (0.08); extend the sweep UP above 0.08.
  - eta: optimum sits on the HIGH boundary (2.0); extend the sweep UP above 2.0.

  - The 80-step 'attraction' (mutual=+0.5) collapses to 0 at 120 steps -> sweep LONGER integration times (e.g. 240, 480, 960 steps) to test whether the deflection grows monotonically or is just lattice jitter.
  - All wins were quarter/half-cell discretised. Try smaller initial separation (e.g. cy2-cy1 = 4 or 5) to amplify the expected 1/r^2 force above the discretisation noise floor.
  - Consider averaging over multiple lut_seeds / sub_seeds so that the apparent attraction is not lut-specific.
  - cutoff_radius was fixed at 4. Sweep it together with alpha/eta — the effective coupling range may need to span more of the inter-particle distance (6 cells).
