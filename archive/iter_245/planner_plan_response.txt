## Analysis

**What we know (confirmed):**
1. LUT-08 is a 4-bit sub-light glider on 3D FCC with O_h-symmetric LUT-08 rule
2. Spatial reflection produces a stable enantiomer with opposite chirality
3. Same-chirality (particle-particle) collisions are 100% elastic across 10 configurations
4. Chirality and sub-lattice parities are additively conserved in same-chirality collisions

**What we DON'T know:**
1. Whether opposite-chirality (particle-antiparticle) collisions produce annihilation vs. elastic scattering
2. Whether the "annihilation" (if it occurs) is clean (vacuum or minimal residuals) vs. messy (debris)
3. Whether the CPT-conjugate glider is bit-conserving during solo propagation (trivially expected but not explicitly verified)
4. Whether any annihilation outcome is O_h-covariant or a lattice-axis artifact

**Critical distinction:** If opposite-chirality collisions ALSO scatter elastically (like same-chirality), then the "antiparticle" label is merely a relabeling of the enantiomer with no physical consequence — a **null result** that must be documented honestly. If instead they annihilate, we have genuine matter-antimatter distinction.

**Construction-vs-Empirical check:** Spatial reflection is a geometric operation, so the existence of the reflected glider is constructional. But the *dynamical difference* between same-chirality and opposite-chirality collisions — if it exists — would be empirical.
