**Script created:** `src/measure_baseline.py`

**What it does:**
1. Generates 100 random C2-symmetric rules with 8 kernel pairs using a fixed seed (150)
2. Evaluates each on a 150×150 soup (25% density, seed=42) for 1600 steps (4 windows × 400)
3. Applies annihilation check (sum of window displacements < 1e-9 → fitness = 0.0)
4. Saves per-rule results to CSV and reports population statistics

**Key finding:** `annihilation_count=0` — no rule in this population completely erased the soup's motion. The typical pattern is a large initial displacement in window 1 that decays to near-zero by window 2-4, which the std_dev penalizes. Rules like `rule_086` (fitness=0.981) show very small, low-variance displacements — the metric rewards rapid settling, not sustained gliding. Evolution must find rules with *constant* non-zero velocity across all windows to score meaningfully above this ~0.82 mean baseline.
